About Me ✨

Hey! I’m building things, learning fast, and having fun doing it.

This section is written in a relaxed, human tone so it feels like a real introduction instead of a formal bio wall.

I’m the kind of student who says yes to interesting ideas. Whether it’s a campus event, a team project, a portfolio build, or a last-minute competition, I love stepping in, figuring things out, and making something cool happen.

I care a lot about design, communication, collaboration, and the feeling a project gives people. I like turning scattered thoughts into something clear, useful, and visually exciting — especially when the work has a story behind it.

Right now, I’m exploring opportunities to grow, learn in public, build a strong body of work, and connect with people who are doing ambitious, creative, and meaningful things.
