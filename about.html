What drives us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make world-class career branding accessible to every ambitious professional — not just those who can afford expensive agencies. We deliver premium-quality resumes, LinkedIn profiles, and personal brands that create real career outcomes.

Every service we offer is built around one metric: does this get our client hired, promoted, or noticed faster than before?

Our Vision

To become the most trusted career identity platform in South Asia — a place where professionals come not just to fix their resume, but to discover their full career narrative.

We envision a future where no skilled professional is ever overlooked because of how their career is presented. Every talent deserves to be seen clearly.

Our journey

How we got here.

2022 — Founded

CVLoom begins as a freelance resume studio.

Started with 3 clients and a single template. The goal was simple: make professional resumes that actually work in the real job market.

2023 — Expansion

LinkedIn branding added. Team grows to 3 specialists.

Demand for LinkedIn profile overhauls led us to build a full branding service. Client satisfaction hit 96% within the first 6 months.

2024 — Platform

Template gallery launched. 200+ clients served.

Built our first template system with 40+ premium layouts. Started serving clients across 10+ industries.

2025 — Studio

Full career identity studio. 500+ professionals served.

Expanded to cover personal branding, portfolio design, and career consulting. Crossed 500 clients with a 98% satisfaction rate.

2026 — Now

Building the next version of CVLoom.

Launching AI-assisted tools, a full template platform, and a career growth ecosystem — while keeping the human quality that defines us.
